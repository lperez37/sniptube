## 0.4.0 (26 September 2026)
- Collection videos become visibly watched after 90% playback, or when marked watched manually.
- Icon actions have comfortable touch targets and list scroll positions return after playback.
- Active sync shows video titles and animated real percentages when sizes are known.
- Settings gains an offline media budget with free-space awareness and an explicit clear-all-local-files action.
- Dark PWA appearance is always used; the theme selector is removed.
- Repeated taps on either side of playback keep adding ten seconds without redundant overlay skip buttons.

## 0.3.0 (26 September 2026)
- First launch asks for a server URL; the app saves it on the phone and does not ship a personal server hostname.
- Branded splash screen and Open web version button in Settings.
- Sync now confirms it is checking collection videos instead of reporting unchanged queue rows as failed work.

## 0.2.0 (26 September 2026)
- Collections always queue their videos for offline use and show known total viewing time.
- Sync now can use cellular after an explicit warning with estimated megabytes and unknown-size items.
- PWA-inspired rounded thumbnails, dark appearance and bottom-pinned bulk controls.
- Search screens give the virtual keyboard space by hiding storage summaries and bulk/navigation chrome while typing.
- Landscape offline playback with double-tap skip and controls that hide during playback.
- About screen with version and release history.

## 0.1.0 (26 September 2026)
- Initial Android companion: search, server-to-phone sync over Wi-Fi, local collections and offline playback.
